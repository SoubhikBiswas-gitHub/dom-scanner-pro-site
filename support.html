Support

  • Contact the developer: use the contact details on the DOM Scanner Pro listing in the Chrome Web Store.
  • Include diagnostics: in the panel, open Settings → Diagnostics for support → Prepare diagnostics. Review the text, then copy it into your message. It contains the extension version, Chrome version, platform, settings and recent error codes, and excludes page addresses, page content, screenshots and reports.
  • Do not include screenshots or reports of private pages unless you have redacted them.

Before reporting, check the limits in the usage guide. Pages that cannot be inspected (Chrome pages, the Web Store, other extensions) are a browser restriction.

DOM Scanner Pro 2.0.4. Runs locally in Chrome, only on pages you choose.
